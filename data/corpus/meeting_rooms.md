# Meeting rooms

Dubai and Bengaluru offices share a room-booking tool: `rooms.northstar.internal`.

## Booking rules

- Rooms hold 15 minutes after the start time, then the booking is released
- Customer rooms (names starting with `C-`) need a calendar invite with an external guest
- Do not book a 12-person room for a 2-person huddle during 10:00–16:00 GST

## Equipment

Every room has HDMI, USB-C, and a conferencing bar. Wireless dongles are at reception. Do not unplug the bar to use a personal speaker.

## After hours

The 8th-floor boardroom in Dubai is locked after 19:00 GST. Security can open it for a customer dinner if Facilities is notified 24 hours ahead.
