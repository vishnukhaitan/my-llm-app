# Annual leave policy

Northstar Labs grants paid annual leave to full-time employees based on years of continuous service.

## Entitlement

- 0 to 2 years of service: 18 working days per calendar year
- 2 to 5 years of service: 22 working days per calendar year
- 5 or more years of service: 25 working days per calendar year

Part-time staff receive a pro-rata entitlement based on contracted hours. Unused leave does not pay out except when employment ends.

## Booking leave

Requests go through the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Managers must respond within 5 working days. Peak product-launch weeks in March and September may be blocked for engineering; HR publishes blocked dates each January.

You may take at most 10 consecutive working days unless People Operations approves a longer stretch in writing.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days may carry into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March. Days above that cap expire on 31 December.

## Public holidays

Northstar observes the UAE public holiday calendar for the Dubai office and the India public holiday calendar for the Bengaluru office. A public holiday that falls during approved annual leave is not deducted from the leave balance.
