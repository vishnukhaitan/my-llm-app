# Work-from-home policy

Northstar Labs is office-first with structured remote days.

## Default pattern

Engineering, design, and product teams work in the office Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday are optional work-from-home days unless a launch review is scheduled.

People Operations, finance, and legal work in the office Monday through Thursday. Friday is optional remote.

## Equipment

Northstar provides one laptop, an external keyboard, and a headset. A home monitor stipend of AED 800 (Dubai) or INR 15,000 (Bengaluru) is available once every 36 months. Submit receipts through the expense policy.

VPN is required for all remote access to internal tools, including the Git server and the internal assistant API. See `vpn_setup.md`.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, you must be reachable on Slack between 11:00 and 16:00 Gulf Standard Time on working days. Stand-ups stay on the team calendar; cameras are optional except for customer calls.

## Exceptions

A fully remote arrangement longer than two consecutive weeks needs director approval and a written coverage plan. New joiners stay office-based for the first 30 calendar days.
