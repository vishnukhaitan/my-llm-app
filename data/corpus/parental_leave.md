# Parental leave

Northstar provides paid parental leave in addition to annual leave.

## Birth parents

16 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base salary. Leave may start up to 2 weeks before the due date. Annual leave does not have to be used first.

## Non-birth parents and adoptive parents

8 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base salary, to be taken within 12 months of birth or placement.

## Notice

Tell People Operations at least 8 weeks before the planned start, or as soon as possible for adoption. A simple email to `people@northstar.example` is enough to start the process; the portal form can follow.

## Returning to work

A phased return of 4 days per week for the first 4 weeks is available on request. Keep medical information in the HR portal, not in Slack.
