# Expense policy

Northstar Labs reimburses reasonable business costs. Submit claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase.

## Receipts

A digital receipt is required for any claim of AED 50 / INR 1,000 or more. Card statements alone are not enough. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Travel

- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Premium economy allowed for flights of 6 hours or more with manager approval
- Hotels: cap AED 700 / INR 12,000 per night unless People Operations pre-approves a city exception

Ride-hailing to and from the airport is reimbursable. Daily commuting is not.

## Software and subscriptions

Team tools must go through IT procurement. Do not expense personal ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, or similar seats; Northstar issues company seats for approved models.

## Processing

Finance pays approved claims every Thursday. Incomplete claims return to the employee and the 30-day clock does not pause.
