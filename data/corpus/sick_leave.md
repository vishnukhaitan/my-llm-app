# Sick leave policy

Northstar Labs provides paid sick leave so employees can recover without using annual leave.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 12 paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

## Certification

- 1 or 2 consecutive calendar days: self-certify in the HR portal on the first day of absence
- 3 or more consecutive calendar days: upload a medical certificate within 48 hours of returning to work

A certificate is also required for any absence on a day immediately before or after a public holiday.

## Pay

Sick leave is paid at 100% of base salary for the first 8 days in a year and at 50% for days 9 through 12. Unpaid sick leave after that requires People Operations approval.

## Infectious illness

If you have a fever or a confirmed infectious illness, do not come to the office. Notify your manager on Slack in the `#team-ops` channel and work from home only if you feel able. Do not attend all-hands while symptomatic.
