# Data retention

Northstar keeps business records only as long as needed for operations and regulation.

## Assistant logs

Questions sent to the internal assistant are stored for 90 days for debugging and cost review, then deleted automatically. Do not paste secrets into questions; logs are visible to platform engineers.

## Email and Slack

Slack public channels are retained for 18 months. Direct messages are retained for 90 days. Email in the company Google Workspace is retained for 3 years unless legal hold applies.

## Code

Git history is retained for the life of the product. Deleted branches on GitHub remain in the object store until garbage collection; do not commit credentials.

## Employee files

People Operations retains contracts for 7 years after the end of employment. Access is limited to People Operations and legal.
