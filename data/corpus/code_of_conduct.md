# Code of conduct

Northstar Labs expects respectful collaboration in offices, Slack, and customer calls.

## Expected behaviour

Give credit for others' work. Review code on the merits. Do not share interview packets outside the hiring panel.

## Harassment

Harassment, discrimination, and threats are prohibited. Report to your manager or to `people@northstar.example`. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

## Conflicts of interest

Paid work for a competitor, or equity in a vendor you manage, must be disclosed to People Operations before it starts.

## Open source

You may contribute to open source on personal time. Do not publish Northstar-internal benchmarks, customer names, or unreleased product names. Use a personal email for personal projects.
